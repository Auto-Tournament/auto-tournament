# Licensing and commercial use

Auto Tournament is licensed under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE). The source is public and free to use, change and share for non-commercial purposes. It is not "open source" in the OSI sense.

## Free, no permission needed

- Personal use, hobby projects, learning and research.
- Running tournaments and LAN events that are free to enter.
- Use by non-profit organizations: charities, schools and universities, public research, health and safety organizations, and government bodies, as the license allows.
- Changing the code, forking it and sharing your changes, as long as you keep the LICENSE and the `Required Notice` line, and your use stays non-commercial.

## Commercial use needs a license from the author

Examples of commercial use:

- Hosting or running Auto Tournament for someone else for money, including managed hosting and "tournaments as a service".
- Selling Auto Tournament, a modified version of it, or a product or service built on it.
- Using it to run tournaments or events that charge players, teams or visitors to take part or attend (entry fees, tickets), unless you are a non-profit organization as listed above.
- Using it inside a business, for example an esports organization, venue or company running events for profit.
- Offering paid setup, installation or operation of Auto Tournament for clients.

Forks and modified versions stay under the same license; they can't be relicensed (for example as MIT) or used commercially without a license.

Not sure whether your use counts as commercial? Ask first.
